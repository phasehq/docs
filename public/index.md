# Introduction

Phase is an open-source, end-to-end encrypted platform for creating, managing, and deploying application secrets and environment variables across your infrastructure.

- **Import** secrets from `.env` files and [inject](https://docs.phase.dev/quickstart.md#3-inject-secrets) them into any application or container at runtime.
- **Automate** secret deployment to platforms like [GitHub Actions](https://docs.phase.dev/integrations/platforms/github-actions.md), [Kubernetes](https://docs.phase.dev/integrations/platforms/kubernetes.md), [AWS](https://docs.phase.dev/integrations/platforms/aws-secrets-manager.md) and more.
- **Share** secrets with your [team](https://docs.phase.dev/console/users.md#add-users-to-an-organisation) and manage access to secrets with [cryptographic RBAC](https://docs.phase.dev/console/environments.md#manage-user-access).
- **Manage** secrets with [environments](https://docs.phase.dev/console/environments.md), audit-logs, [RBAC](https://docs.phase.dev/access-control.md) etc.

![console](https://docs.phase.dev/assets/images/console/console-ui.webp)

The Phase platform provides powerful secret management features including personal secret overrides, secret versioning & roll-back, secret referencing, RBAC, and much more.
You can replace .env files with runtime secret injection with a fully-featured [CLI](https://docs.phase.dev/cli.md), deploy secrets to third party services with native integrations, and build your own custom workflows and integrations with [SDKs](https://docs.phase.dev/sdks.md) and [APIs](https://docs.phase.dev/public-api.md).

The platform is built on a sophisticated [encryption architecture](https://docs.phase.dev/security/architecture.md), while abstracting as much of the complexity of this implementation away from the user as possible.
