# Environments

Environments in Phase allow you to manage your application secrets through various stages of your development process. This guide explains how to create, update, and manage environments in your Phase application.

<DocActions /> 

When you create a new application in Phase, it is automatically initialized with three default environments:

- Development
- Staging
- Production

These default environments provide a standard structure for managing your application secrets across different stages of your development workflow. You can then customize these or add additional environments as needed.

<Note>
 The ability to create and manage custom environments is available for organizations with a `Phase Pro` or `Enterprise` tier subscription.
</Note>

Environment names must match the pattern `^[a-zA-Z0-9\-_]{1,32}$`. This means they can only contain:
- Lowercase letters (a-z)
- Uppercase letters (A-Z)
- Numbers (0-9)
- Hyphens (-)
- Underscores (_)
- Must be between 1 and 32 characters long

## Creating a New Environment

To create a new environment:

1. Navigate to the Secrets overview screen inside your app in the Phase console.
2. Click the "+ New Environment" button.
![Create a new environment button](/assets/images/console/environments/new-environment-button.webp)
3. In the popup dialog, you'll see a message: "Create a new Environment in this App". Enter a name for your new environment in the "Environment name" field. For example, you might enter "QA" for a quality assurance environment.
![Create a new environment](/assets/images/console/environments/create-new-environment.webp)
4. Click `Create`

Note: All Organisation Admins will have access to this Environment.

## Managing Existing Environments

To manage an existing environment:

1. On the Secrets overview screen, locate the environment card you want to manage.
2. Click on the cog icon (⚙️) at the top right of the environment card.

![Manage environment cog button](/assets/images/console/environments/manage-environment-cog-button.webp) 

3. A "Manage [Environment Name]" dialog will appear with several options:

![Manage existing environment](/assets/images/console/environments/update-environment.webp)


### Renaming an Environment

1. Enter the new name in the "Environment name" field.
2. Click the `Rename` button to apply the change.

<Warning>
Changing the environment name will affect how you construct references to secrets.
</Warning>

### Managing Access

1. In the "Environment Members" section, you can see users who have access to Secrets in this Environment.
2. Click `Manage access` to add or remove user access to this environment.

![Manage environment access dialog](/assets/images/console/environments/environment-manage-access.webp)

### Deleting an Environment

Deleting an Environment locks it at once. After 24 hours, Phase permanently deletes the Environment and all Secrets and Integrations associated with it. Until then, you can restore it. You can also [delete it immediately](#deleting-an-environment-immediately).

1. At the bottom of the dialog, you'll find a "Delete Environment" section. Click the `Delete` button.
2. The confirmation dialog shows when the Environment will be deleted. Type the Environment name to confirm, then click `Schedule for deletion`.

![Delete environment confirmation](/assets/images/console/environments/environment-delete.webp)

<Warning>
Once an Environment has been permanently deleted, this can't be undone.
</Warning>

<Note>
To delete or restore an Environment that contains [rotating secrets](/console/rotating-secrets), you also need permission to delete rotating secrets.
</Note>

#### Scheduled for Deletion

For the next 24 hours, the Environment is locked:

- It leaves the App's list of Environments. It shows under "Scheduled for deletion" on the App's Home tab, with its deletion time.
- Its Secrets can't be read or changed from the Console, the CLI, the API or the SDKs.
- Its secret syncs and secret rotations pause.
- Its name stays reserved. You can't create another Environment with that name in the App, or rename one to it, until the Environment has been deleted.

![Environment scheduled for deletion on the App's Home tab](/assets/images/console/environments/environment-scheduled-for-deletion.webp)

If you change a member's, service account's or team's access to the App's Environments in the meantime, they lose their access to this Environment, and a restore doesn't bring it back.

#### Restoring an Environment

To restore an Environment, click `Restore` next to it under "Scheduled for deletion". Anyone who can delete the Environment can restore it.

After a restore, the Environment works as before, and its paused syncs and rotations resume.

#### Deleting an Environment Immediately

To skip the 24 hours, tick "Delete immediately" in the confirmation dialog. The button changes to `Delete now`. Phase starts deleting the Environment right away, and it can't be restored. Its name can be used again once it is gone.

![Delete an environment immediately](/assets/images/console/environments/environment-delete-immediately.webp)

For an Environment that is already scheduled for deletion, click `Delete now` next to it under "Scheduled for deletion".

#### Permanent Deletion

Phase deletes an Environment the way it deletes an App. It revokes active dynamic secret leases and rotating secret credentials at the provider, stops the Environment's syncs, deletes its data, and emails the organisation's Owners and the member who deleted it. If a lease or credential can't be revoked, the deletion is delayed. See [Permanent deletion](/console/apps#permanent-deletion) and [Delayed deletion](/console/apps#delayed-deletion) for what happens then.

Deleting an [App](/console/apps#delete-an-app) deletes all of its Environments with it.

Remember, Organization Owners and Admins have access to all Environments by default. For Developers, you can control Environment access from the App Members screen.

By leveraging these customizable Environments, you can create a workflow that suits your development process, from testing and staging to production deployments.
