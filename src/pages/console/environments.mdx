# Environments

Environments in Phase allow you to manage your application secrets through various stages of your development process. This guide explains how to create, update, and manage environments in your Phase application.

<DocActions /> 

When you create a new application in Phase, it is automatically initialized with three default environments:

- Development
- Staging
- Production

These default environments provide a standard structure for managing your application secrets across different stages of your development workflow. You can then customize these or add additional environments as needed.

<Note>
 The ability to create and manage custom environments is available for organizations with a `Phase Pro` or `Enterprise` tier subscription.
</Note>

Environment names must match the pattern `^[a-zA-Z0-9\-_]{1,32}$`. This means they can only contain:
- Lowercase letters (a-z)
- Uppercase letters (A-Z)
- Numbers (0-9)
- Hyphens (-)
- Underscores (_)
- Must be between 1 and 32 characters long

## Creating a New Environment

To create a new environment:

1. Navigate to the **Home** tab of your App in the Phase Console.
2. Open the "New Secret" menu and click "New Environment". You can also click the "+" column at the end of the secrets editor.
![Create a new environment button](/assets/images/console/environments/new-environment-button.webp)
3. In the popup dialog, you'll see a message: "Create a new Environment in this App". Enter a name for your new environment in the "Environment name" field. For example, you might enter "QA" for a quality assurance environment. You can click the color swatch next to the name to choose the color that marks this environment in the secrets editor.
4. Under "Access", select the members and service accounts that should have access to the new environment.
![Create a new environment](/assets/images/console/environments/create-new-environment.webp)
5. Click `Create`

Note: All Organisation Admins will have access to this Environment.

The new environment is added as a column at the end of the [secrets editor](/console/secrets#working-across-environments).

### Cloning an Environment

You can create a new environment that starts with a copy of the secrets and folders of an existing one. This is useful for adding another environment, such as a second region or a preview environment, without having to enter every secret again.

1. On the **Home** tab of your App, open the options menu in the column header of the environment you want to copy, and click "Clone environment". Alternatively, choose the environment under "Clone from" when creating a new environment.
![Environment options menu with the Clone environment option](/assets/images/console/environments/environment-column-menu.webp)
2. Enter a name for the new environment and choose who should have access to it. The members and service accounts that have access to the environment you are cloning are selected by default.
![Clone an environment](/assets/images/console/environments/environment-clone.webp)
3. Click `Create`

<Note>
Sealed secrets, rotating secrets, dynamic secrets, and personal secret overrides are not copied to the new environment.
</Note>

You can also start a new environment from just a few secrets: [select the secrets](/console/secrets#select-multiple-secrets) in the secrets editor and click "New Environment".

## Managing Existing Environments

To manage an existing environment:

1. On the **Home** tab of your App, hover over the column header of the environment you want to manage.
2. Click on the cog icon (⚙️) that appears in the column header.

![Manage environment cog button](/assets/images/console/environments/manage-environment-cog-button.webp) 

3. A "Manage [Environment Name]" dialog will appear with several options:

![Manage existing environment](/assets/images/console/environments/update-environment.webp)


### Renaming an Environment

1. Enter the new name in the "Environment name" field. You can also change the color of the environment by clicking the color swatch next to its name.
2. Click the `Save` button to apply the change.

<Warning>
Changing the environment name will affect how you construct references to secrets.
</Warning>

### Reordering Environments

Environments are shown as columns in the secrets editor. To change their order, drag an environment by the grip in its column header, or open the options menu in the column header and click "Move left" or "Move right".

### Managing Access

1. In the "Environment Members" section, you can see users who have access to Secrets in this Environment.
2. Click `Manage access` to add or remove user access to this environment.

![Manage environment access dialog](/assets/images/console/environments/environment-manage-access.webp)

### Deleting an Environment

1. At the bottom of the dialog, you'll find a "Delete Environment" section.
2. Be aware that deleting an environment will permanently delete this Environment and all associated Secrets and Integrations.
3. Click the `Delete` button, then type the name of the environment to confirm. Use this option with caution.

![Delete environment section and confirmation](/assets/images/console/environments/environment-delete.webp)

Remember, Organization Owners and Admins have access to all Environments by default. For Developers, you can control Environment access from the App Members screen.

By leveraging these customizable Environments, you can create a workflow that suits your development process, from testing and staging to production deployments.
